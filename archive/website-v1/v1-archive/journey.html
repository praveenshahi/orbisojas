The Journey

Five levels of descent. You enter where you are — in your mind. The work takes you inward.

Level One Level Two Level Three Level Four Level Five
Level One

Recognition

If you are here: you can explain your patterns with precision. You have done the reading — the psychology, the philosophy, possibly the spirituality. You understand what's wrong. The understanding hasn't made it stop.

The work at this level is the first felt sense that something beneath the intellect is alive. Not another idea about yourself — the beginning of actually feeling what you've been thinking about.

What becomes available: the recognition that understanding was never the problem. And the Mirror — the first experience of being seen beneath the performance.

Container: Mirror + community access

Level Two

The Emotional Body

If you are here: you've begun to feel what you only understood. The emotional body is emerging — not as a concept, but as sensation. The wound is no longer just a story. It's something you feel in your chest, your gut, the base of your spine.

The work at this level is learning to stay with what surfaces. Not to fix it, analyse it, or make it productive. To feel it fully — possibly for the first time.

What becomes available: the first genuine contact with the preverbal material — where the felt sense of the wound, not the story about it, is met.

Container: Blueprint — personal architecture session

Level Three

The Vital Field

If you are here: breathwork and somatic work are no longer techniques. They are reaching the layer where the wound actually lives. The Pranamaya layer — the felt sense of being alive — is coming back online.

The work at this level is the lineage encounter — recognising the ancestral pattern as not-self. Distinguishing between what was inherited and what is authentically yours.

What becomes available: vitality returning as the suppression patterns release. The body beginning to feel like a place worth living in.

Container: Circle — 8-week cohort

Level Four

The Body as Home

If you are here: the body is no longer maintained for performance. It is being inhabited. The physical layer is becoming the first instrument of presence rather than the last casualty of optimisation.

The work at this level is releasing accumulated attainment into embodied presence. Letting go of the identity built around having done the work.

What becomes available: genuine embodied sovereignty. Not performed. Not understood. Lived.

Container: Intensive — 3–5 days, 12 participants

Level Five

The Ground

Not a destination. A discovery. What remains when the layers above it stop performing.

The ground beneath all other layers. Already present. The consciousness layer that was never a goal to reach — only a remembering that happens when everything above it becomes quiet.

What becomes available: the capacity to transmit what was received. Not through teaching. Through the quality of your own presence.

Container: Guide Path

The journey begins with the Mirror.

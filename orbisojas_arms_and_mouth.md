## SECTION ONE — THE BRAND

### 1.1 What Orbisojas Is

**In one sentence:**
Orbisojas maps the patterns you inherited — so you can see what's yours and what isn't.

**In one paragraph:**
Most people spend years trying to change patterns they didn't choose, using tools that reach the part of them that already understands. Orbisojas works below understanding — in the body, in the felt sense, in the layer where the pattern actually lives. It is not therapy. It is not coaching. It is not spirituality. It is a precise map of the architecture beneath the life you've built — and a guided descent into it.

**What it is not:**
Not therapy. Not coaching. Not healing (at entry — healing is what happens; it is not the promise made). Not a wellness platform. Not spiritual practice in the traditional sense. Not self-help.

The distinction matters and must be held precisely in all participant-facing language. The ICP — especially Presentation One (the Pure Mind) — has already done therapy and found it insufficient. Framing Orbisojas as therapy loses them before the first sentence lands.

### 1.2 The Category

**Inherited Architecture**

This is the category Orbisojas occupies and, for now, defines alone.

Inherited Architecture means: the patterns governing your life — the way you relate, the way you work, the way you feel (or don't), the way your body holds tension, the way shame moves through you without your permission — these are not character flaws. They are structural. They predate you. They were designed by people who didn't know they were designing them, inherited by you before you had the capacity to choose differently.

Seeing the architecture is the first act of freedom. You cannot redesign what you cannot see.

**The category claim:**
*"You inherited more than their eyes."*

**The positioning line:**
*"Orbisojas — Inherited Architecture."*

### 1.3 The One Who Finds Orbisojas

Two presentations. One underlying condition.

**The Pure Mind:**
High-functioning. Intellectually sophisticated. Has done the reading — psychology, philosophy, possibly spirituality. Can explain their patterns with precision. Cannot stop them. Lives from the neck up. The body is maintained for performance. Feelings are understood, not felt. The void is real but unnamed. The therapy helped them understand what's wrong. It didn't make it stop.

*What they say:* "I've done the therapy, read the books, still feel the same."

**The Body-But-Wound:**
Has found the body through gym, yoga, breathwork, cold exposure, some combination. Is in the body — or appears to be. The inner child wound did not dissolve. It became more disciplined. They feel better but not freer. Something is still running on autopilot. They can't name what.

*What they say:* "I've done the work. I know my patterns. They're still here."

**What both share:**
The void that cannot be named. The language gap. They feel something that has no precise word in the vocabulary they've been given. When Orbisojas gives them that word — that sentence, that precise reflection — the recognition is immediate and physical. That moment of recognition is the conversion. Not a sales page. The recognition itself.

### 1.4 The Voice

**Always:**
- Start in the participant's reality. Not the framework's reality.
- Plain language until the framework has been earned through recognition.
- One idea per piece. Fully inhabited.
- Leave space. Not every line needs to work hard.
- The depth is transmitted, not explained.

**Never:**
- Sanskrit terms in the first encounter with any participant.
- "Healing" language at entry.
- Explanation of what Orbisojas is before the participant has felt what Orbisojas addresses.
- Performing depth. Either it transmits or it waits.
- Any line that sounds like it came from a content strategy meeting.

**The test for any piece of writing:**
Would a person who has never heard of Orbisojas feel precisely seen by this? If yes — it's ready. If it requires prior knowledge of the framework to land — it's not ready for external use.

### 1.5 The Visual Language

**Direction:** Deep, serious, warm. The feeling of entering a quiet room where something important is about to be said.

**Color:** Deep terracotta, dark ochre, near-black, warm stone. Not wellness pastels. Not tech dark mode. Something that has been here longer than a trend.

**Typography:** Literary serif for body — something with genuine character. A display face that is distinctive without being decorative. The typography should feel like a serious book, not a startup landing page.

**Imagery (if used):** Architectural cross-sections. Topographic maps. Geological layers. Root systems. The visual language of what lies beneath the surface. Nothing stock. No meditation poses. No mandala. No lotus. No floating orbs.

**Texture:** Grain, paper texture, depth — over gradients and flatness. The platform should feel material, not digital.

**The single test:** Does this look like something that has been here for a while and will be here long after trends pass?

The platform is minimal. Five pages. No blog at launch. No about page in third person. No testimonials before the first cohort. The founder's voice is in the content, not in a bio.

**Page 1 — Home**

One job: make the ICP feel seen before explaining anything.

Structure:
- **Headline (above fold):** Names their reality. Not what Orbisojas is.
  - Option A: *"You inherited more than their eyes."*
  - Option B: *"The pattern keeps repeating. You've tried to stop it. There's a reason it hasn't."*
  - Option C: *"You built everything they said would make you feel whole. Why doesn't it?"*

- **Subheadline:** Proposes there is a structure beneath what they feel.
  - *"Orbisojas maps the architecture beneath the patterns you can't stop repeating."*

- **Three reality statements (below headline, above fold or just below):**
  Three short descriptions of the ICP's lived experience. No framework language.
  - *"You can explain your patterns with precision. You can't stop them."*
  - *"You've done the work. Read the books. Something is still running underneath."*
  - *"The achievement landed. The emptiness didn't leave."*

- **Single CTA:** "Begin the Mirror"

- **Below fold:** What the Mirror is, in two sentences. What happens after. No product list. No pricing. Not yet.

- **No navigation to framework pages at entry.** The homepage leads to the Mirror. The Mirror leads to everything else.

**Page 2 — The Mirror**

What it is. How it works. What the participant receives.

Structure:
- **Opening:** *"The Mirror is not a quiz. It is a conversation — designed to reflect back the architecture beneath what you're carrying. Most people describe it as the first time they've felt precisely seen."*
- **The three questions, displayed without explanation.** Let them land.
- **What the participant receives:** A Mirror output document. Wound type. The pattern in the body. The timing layer. The recommended next step.
- **Time:** 20-30 minutes. Designed to be entered when there is space for it.
- **CTA:** "Begin"
- **Note on the birth data:** *"We ask for your date and place of birth. We'll explain what we do with it inside the Mirror — after the first two questions have done their work."*

**Page 3 — The Journey**

The five levels described in plain language. Participant-experience first. Framework language earns its place in descriptions of later levels.

Structure — each level described in this format:
- What the participant is experiencing when they enter this level
- What the work of this level actually is
- What becomes available when this level completes
- The product container for this level

Level descriptions written as: *"If you are [specific experience], this is where you are. The work at this level is [plain language]. What becomes available: [plain language]."*

Framework language (Kosha names, Vayu terms) appears in Level 3 onward — earned by the participant's experience of Levels 1 and 2.

**Page 4 — The Framework**

For participants who want to understand the intellectual architecture. Not linked from the homepage. Linked from: the Mirror output, the Journey page Level 3+ descriptions, the community.

This page contains:
- The Inherited Architecture category explanation
- The Kosha descent model (with plain English equivalents for every term)
- The Vayu-wound mapping (in felt-body language first, technical language available)
- The Jyotish layer explained as timing, not prediction
- The distinction from therapy/coaching/spirituality

Every Sanskrit term has a plain English equivalent within the same sentence or as a hover/parenthetical. The technical language is available for those who want precision. It is never required.

**Page 5 — Begin**

The Mirror entry. Clean. Name, date of birth, location of birth. One opening question before the Mirror begins. No distractions.

### 2.2 The Sanskrit Accessibility Protocol

Every Sanskrit term used anywhere on the platform has a plain English equivalent available within one sentence. Not a glossary page. Not a footnote. In the sentence itself.

Format examples:
- *"Pranamaya (the felt sense of being alive — the layer where vitality lives and where the preverbal wound is encoded)"*
- *"Apana Vayu (the downward, grounding movement of energy — when this reverses, it encodes as shame in the belly and base)"*
- *"Vijnanamaya (the discriminative intelligence — the layer that analyses, compares, performs understanding)"*

The Sanskrit is the precise language. The English equivalent is the accessible door. Both live on the platform. The participant chooses which language they're ready for. Neither is privileged over the other.

For the 18-24 ICP: the English equivalent leads. The Sanskrit is available for the curious. Never required.
For the 25-35 ICP: both offered simultaneously. The Sanskrit signals seriousness and precision.
For the global audience: the English equivalent is complete in itself. The Sanskrit is context, not requirement.

## SECTION THREE — THE CONTENT SYSTEM

### 3.1 The Two-Layer Architecture

**Layer 1 — Transmission Pieces**
Written by the founder from genuine Pranamaya state — after sadhana, from the place where something is actually alive. These are the pieces that produce the "how did you know this about me" response. They cannot be manufactured or scheduled. They happen when they happen.

Volume: 2-4 per week. Not daily. These are the pieces that matter most and do the most work.

**Layer 2 — Framework Articulations**
Intelligent articulations of the framework — the five Entry Doors, the three Mirror questions in rotation, the two-register versions of core themes. The content agent produces a first draft. The founder reads, approves or revises, publishes. This is not transmission. It is accurate representation of the framework with the founder's review as quality control.

The founder's daily review of Layer 2 pieces: 15 minutes. Sustainable. The content agent handles formatting, scheduling, platform-specific adaptation.

**The rule:** Layer 1 pieces are never produced from content-strategy brain. Layer 2 pieces are never posted without the founder's review. The distinction between them is maintained explicitly and always.

### 3.2 The Five Entry Doors

Each door reaches a different segment of the same ICP. All five in rotation.

**Door 1 — The System Door**
For the person who sees the exploitation but can't name their internal response to it.

Hook pattern: *"The system didn't fail you. It worked exactly as designed. The question is what it designed you to feel about yourself."*

Topics: hollow achievement, corporate identity, financial anxiety, the career that feels like a performance

**Door 2 — The Relationship Door**
For the person whose relationships feel like transactions or ego management.

Hook pattern: *"You're not bad at relationships. You're good at performing them. Those are opposite problems."*

Topics: loneliness inside connection, intimacy avoidance, the partner as completion-seeking, why vulnerability feels dangerous

**Door 3 — The Body Door**
For the person who lives from the neck up — and for the person who found the body but brought the wound with them.

Hook pattern: *"Your body has been keeping score of everything your mind refused to feel."*

For Presentation Two specifically: *"You've been in the body for years. The conditioning is still running. Not because the practice failed — because the practice never reached where the conditioning lives."*

Topics: anxiety in the chest, shame in the gut, the body as instrument vs body as home, why harder practice doesn't dissolve the wound

**Door 4 — The Parent Door**
For the person ready to look at lineage.

Hook pattern: *"You didn't inherit your parents' trauma. You inherited their unfinished business. There's a difference — and the difference is where the work begins."*

Topics: generational patterns, why you repeat what you hated, shame that doesn't feel like yours, the child who learned to disappear

**Door 5 — The World Door**
For the person who sees the macro chaos and feels it internally.

Hook pattern: *"The chaos outside is the chaos inside. Same system. Same architecture. Same medicine."*

Topics: the digital economy as Pranamaya suppression mechanism, consciousness as the battlefield, what it means to opt out without retreating, building genuine vitality in a performance economy

### 3.3 The Three Mirror Questions as Content

These three questions are the content engine. Each is a standalone post, a reel hook, a Circle opening. Posted one at a time. Nothing else. No explanation. No Orbisojas mention.

> *"What did you know about yourself as a child that the world taught you was wrong?"*

> *"Where in your body do you feel the weight of something that doesn't belong to you?"*

> *"What would you do if you knew the shame wasn't yours?"*

Rotate monthly. The comments are market research and the next 30 days of content. High-resonance responses — those that share specifically, that go personal rather than general — are flagged for founder direct engagement. These are the Mirror conversation candidates.

### 3.4 The Two-Register System

The same framework, two containers. Not two brands. One framework with two entry languages.

| | Register 1 — 25-35 | Register 2 — 18-24 |
|---|---|---|
| Entry wound | Hollow achievement — built the life, can't inhabit it | Identity confusion — performing authenticity while feeling empty |
| Language | Philosophical, willing to sit with complexity, reads long-form | Brief, punchy, metaphor-led. Depth available but not leading. |
| Platform | LinkedIn, Substack, long-form | Instagram Reels, short-form video, Twitter/X threads |
| Conversion path | Mirror → Blueprint → Circle | Mirror → community → Circle when ready |

**Example — same theme, two registers:**

*Theme: The Inherited Shame*

[25-35] The shame you carry around your genuine desire is not yours. It encoded before you had the language to question it — when someone else's unresolved contraction became what you believed you yourself felt. You can give it back. Not through understanding that you should. Through touching the place in the body where it still lives as sensation, and meeting it there.

[18-24] That cringe you feel about wanting things? That's not you. That's your parents' fear of wanting things living in your nervous system. They learned desire was dangerous. You absorbed it as fact. It isn't yours. You can give it back — but not by thinking about it. By feeling where it actually is.

Every core Layer 1 piece written in one register gets a Layer 2 adaptation for the other register. Founder reviews both.

### 3.5 Platform-Specific Formatting

**LinkedIn**
- Opening line: statement of truth. No question. No "I." Bold enough to stop the scroll.
- Length: 150-300 words. One idea. White space.
- No hashtags in body. Three max at end.
- End: open question OR silence. Never a CTA.

**Instagram / Reels**
- Hook: under 8 words. Stops the thumb.
- Body: 3-5 lines. Each line breathes.
- No explanation. Trust the resonance.
- Reels: speak to one person. Not an audience.

**Long-form (Substack)**
- Opens at the macro (world, system, pattern)
- Moves to the micro (body, wound, lineage)
- Framework reveals itself through the journey, not the introduction
- Ends with the opening — what becomes possible — not a conclusion
- Orbisojas named briefly at the end, as "this is the work" not "this is the product"

**The quality check before any piece goes live:**
- Does the first line meet the person where they are, not where Orbisojas is?
- Is framework language absent from the first half?
- Is there one idea, fully inhabited?
- Does it feel like transmission or content strategy?
- Is there space — or is every line working too hard?
- Would someone who has never heard of Orbisojas feel seen by this?
- Is there any selling happening? (If yes — remove it.)

## SECTION FOUR — THE PARTICIPANT JOURNEY

### 4.1 Five Moments

**Moment 1 — Discovery**
The person encounters Orbisojas through content, word of mouth, or search. They are not looking for Orbisojas. They are looking for what Orbisojas addresses.

Acquisition language never names the framework. It names the participant's reality.

Search-facing language examples:
- "The pattern keeps repeating. The therapy didn't stop it."
- "You built everything they said would make you feel whole. Why doesn't it?"
- "The weight you're carrying — some of it isn't yours."

Social content: Entry Door pieces and Mirror questions. The Viveka filter operating in public.

**Moment 2 — Landing**
They arrive at orbisojas.com. 8-12 seconds before they stay or leave.

The homepage makes them feel seen before explaining anything. Single CTA. No framework explanation. Not yet.

**Moment 3 — The Mirror**
First genuine Orbisojas experience. 20-30 minutes. AI-powered, structured conversation.

The three questions, one at a time, with space. Not a form — a conversation. After each answer: the Mirror reflects in felt-body language. Not analysis. Precision recognition.

The output: wound type, Vayu pattern, dasha layer, recommended next step. The output is the conversion mechanic — not a sales page. The output itself makes the next step obvious.

**Moment 4 — The Journey**
Mirror output routes the participant to their entry point:

- **Blueprint** (personal session): for participants whose Mirror indicates readiness for 1:1 depth work
- **Circle** (group cohort): for participants ready for a community container
- **Community** (free): for participants at the beginning of pattern recognition who need more time in the field before the paid work

Each path described in the output as: *"Based on what you've shared, here is what the next step looks like for someone where you are."* Not a product menu. A genuine recommendation.

**Moment 5 — The Return**
Every participant who completes a Circle or Blueprint is invited to the next level through a closing conversation — not a sales email. The journey continues until the participant is ready to become a guide, or until they have what they came for and leave complete.

No chasing. No urgency. The work is real. It will wait for the right person at the right time.

### 4.2 The Mirror Conversation Architecture

The Mirror runs as a structured AI conversation. The founder writes all reflection language and reviews outputs periodically for quality and accuracy. The Mirror improves with every session — the accumulating dataset of dasha-wound correlations is the compounding asset.

**The conversation structure:**

*Opening:* "Before we begin — your name, and the date and place you were born. We'll tell you what we do with this inside the Mirror, after the first two questions have done their work."

*Question 1:* Displayed in full. Space given. The Mirror reflects back what it heard — in the participant's language, not the framework's. No interpretation yet.

*Question 2:* The body question. After the participant answers, the Mirror names the body location and maps it to the Vayu pattern — but in felt-body language, not technical language. *"The weight in [body region] — that's not random. That's where [type of wound] lives in the body."*

*Question 3:* The hypothetical. After the answer, the Mirror identifies the suppression architecture — what the shame has been preventing, and whose it was before it became the participant's.

*The Jyotish layer:* After the three questions have done their work and the participant has already felt seen twice, the Mirror introduces the timing layer. *"Your birth data tells us something specific about why this material is surfacing now with this intensity — and when this window closes. Here is what your chart shows."*

*The output:* Generated as a document the participant can keep. Wound type. Vayu pattern. Dasha timing. Recommended entry. Written in plain language with framework terms available for those who want them.

### 4.3 The Blueprint Session

The Blueprint is a 90-minute personal session — founder-delivered for the first 50, then guide-delivered with the curriculum documented.

Structure:
- 20 minutes: Mirror output as entry point. What did the participant experience? What landed? What resisted?
- 30 minutes: The wound architecture mapped in depth. The developmental window. The ancestral pattern. The Vayu disturbance felt in the body during the conversation.
- 20 minutes: The Jyotish layer in depth. The dasha. The chart signature. The timing.
- 20 minutes: The path forward. Specific to this participant. Not a product pitch — a genuine recommendation for what this person needs next.

The Blueprint output: a personal architecture document the participant keeps. Their wound type, Vayu profile, dasha context, and the recommended path — in writing.

### 4.4 The Circle

Eight weeks. Cohort-based. 8-12 participants. Demographic-separated — 25-35 cohort and 18-24 cohort facilitated separately.

The Circle curriculum is documented before the first Circle runs. What happens in each of the eight weeks. What the participants are moving through. What the facilitator's role is. What progress looks like — in observable terms, not only in the facilitator's felt sense.

**The eight-week arc:**

Weeks 1-2: Recognition. The group sees the architecture. Naming what has been unnamed. The collective field beginning to form.

Weeks 3-4: Descent. The breathwork and somatic work reaching the Pranamaya layer. The felt sense of the wound, not the story about it.

Weeks 5-6: The Lineage Encounter. The ancestral material. Whose wound this was. The moment of genuine distinction between inherited material and authentic self.

Weeks 7-8: The Opening. What becomes available when the inherited weight is seen clearly. Not resolution — opening. The participants leave with their wound map and the beginning of genuine Pranamaya vitality.

## SECTION SIX — THE GUIDE

### 6.1 The Guide's Prerequisites

Not credentials. Not technique. One thing: the guide has made the descent themselves.

They have been in their own Pranamaya layer — not as a concept, as an experience. They have met their wound material, not only understood it. They carry the Orbisojas transmission frequency because they have been through what they are facilitating. This is the only qualification that cannot be faked and cannot be substituted.

The guide training develops: facilitation skills, Mirror interpretation capacity, Jyotish diagnostic basics, breathwork and somatic toolkit, Circle curriculum delivery, quality standard recognition.

The guide's role in the Circle: hold the field. Not perform wisdom. Not deliver content. Hold the Pranamaya presence that allows the participants' own Pranamaya to surface safely.

### 6.2 What the Guide Never Does

- Facilitates before completing at least Level 3 of their own journey
- Delivers the Mirror without genuine presence — not every session requires peak state, but the guide must be genuinely in their body, not performing it
- Names the wound type before the participant has felt it — the participant arrives at the name through recognition, not diagnosis
- Treats the framework as complete — it is a living system that develops through genuine contact with the material it addresses

### 6.3 The Quality Standard — Observable Markers

Before any guide delivery or content piece:

**Observable:** Is any Sanskrit term in the opening without an English equivalent? (Remove or add equivalent)

**Observable:** Is there more than one idea in this piece or session focus? (If yes — choose one)

**Observable:** Does the first line/opening question meet the participant in their reality, not the framework's? (If not — start again)

**Requires judgment (founder review):** Does it feel like transmission or content strategy? Is there a pulse in it, or only structure?

The first three are checkable by anyone. The fourth is where the founder's review is genuinely needed. Narrow the irreplaceable function to the genuinely irreplaceable decisions.

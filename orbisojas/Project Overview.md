# Project Overview

## What Orbisojas Is

**One sentence:** Orbisojas maps the patterns you inherited — so you can see what's yours and what isn't.

**One paragraph:** Most people spend years trying to change patterns they didn't choose, using tools that reach the part of them that already understands. Orbisojas works below understanding — in the body, in the felt sense, in the layer where the pattern actually lives. It is not therapy. It is not coaching. It is not spirituality. It is a precise map of the architecture beneath the life you've built — and a guided descent into it.

## What It Is Not

Not therapy. Not coaching. Not a wellness platform. Not spiritual practice in the traditional sense. Not self-help. Not self-improvement. It's self-excavation.

## The Founding Thesis — The Inversion

Every transformation framework in history was built to go upward — body toward spirit, dense toward subtle. The classical path says: "you are too much in the body. Rise."

Orbisojas inverts this. The modern human (urban, digitally-native, 18-38) is catastrophically disembodied. They live in the intellectual and emotional layers with such dominance that the body has become a foreign country.

**The Orbisojas thesis:** You are not in the body at all. Descend.

- Every other brand designs UP (light, airy, aspirational). Orbisojas designs DOWN.
- The way out is through. The way through is down.
- Not transcendence. Return.

## Target Audience

Gen Z (18-28 primary), digitally native, intellectually sophisticated. Two core presentations:

1. **The Overthinker** — Read the books, did the therapy, can explain their attachment style perfectly. Nothing changed. Understanding was never the problem.
2. **The Disciplined One** — Found the body (gym, breathwork, cold plunges), but brought the wound with them. The anxiety just has better posture now.

## The Mirror

The core product. A diagnostic conversation — three questions, twenty minutes.

**Q1:** "What did you know about yourself as a child — before the world taught you it was wrong?"
**Q2:** "Where in your body do you feel the weight of something that doesn't belong to you?"
**Q3:** "What would you do if you knew the shame wasn't yours?"

The entire website is designed to lead to one button: "Enter the Mirror."

## The Journey Structure

The website presents two parallel descent journeys — one for boys, one for girls — each as a 4-scene scroll-driven pixel art narrative.

| Scene | Theme | Copy Hook |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| 01 | The Surface — Maps given to you | "Everyone gave you a map. Nobody told you it wasn't yours." |
| 02 | The Crack — Something breaks | Societal expectations fracturing |
| 03 | The Descent — Going inward | "The layer you've been skipping" |
| 04 | The Ground — Arriving home | "Not something you achieve. Something you remember." |

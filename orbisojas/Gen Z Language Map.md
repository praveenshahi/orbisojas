## The Two Presentations

### The Overthinker (Pure Mind)
You've read the books. Done the therapy. You can explain your attachment style, name your trauma responses, map your childhood wounds with clinical precision. You understand *everything* about yourself. And nothing has changed.

**The line:** "You can explain your patterns perfectly. You can't stop them. That gap? That's where we work."

### The Disciplined One (Body-But-Wound)
Cold plunges. Breathwork. Gym at 5am. You *look* like someone who's done the work. But the anxiety is still there. The shame just has better posture now.

**The line:** "You've been in the body for years. The conditioning is still running. Not because the practice failed — because it never reached where the conditioning lives."

## The Wound Types

### The Mask (Wounded Bhairava)
Performing strength because the alternative — being soft, being seen — feels like dying. "You're not strong. You're defended."

### The Filter (Wounded Bhairavi)
Every expression edited before it leaves you. "You're not fake. You're protecting something real that was once punished for being real."

### The Missing Piece (Projected Wholeness)
Looking for completion in someone else. "Not because you're broken. Because nobody taught you that the missing piece was always inside you."

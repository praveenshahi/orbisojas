# Design System

> Design is not decoration applied to content. Design IS the content's first language.
> Before a single word is read, the design has already spoken.

Full reference doc: `../orbisojas_design_system.md`

---

## Core Principle: Design as Descent

Every design decision answers one question: **does this take the viewer deeper, or does it keep them on the surface?**

- Flat layouts keep you on the surface. Depth pulls you in.
- Bright, even lighting keeps you on the surface. Chiaroscuro pulls you in.
- Text explaining something keeps you on the surface. Text naming what you already feel pulls you in.

**The Inversion Applied:** Every wellness brand designs UP (light, airy, aspirational). Orbisojas designs DOWN. Light comes from below. Depth increases as you scroll. Nothing floats — everything has gravity. The ground is the warmest place, not the sky.

## What the Design Must Feel Like

Not "look like." Feel like.

1. **The 2am phone realization** — lying in the dark, phone is the only light, something true arrives uninvited
2. **The childhood drawing** — direct, unpolished, pre-performance. Pixel art carries this quality.
3. **The old book found in a trunk** — timeless because it never tried to be timely

## Spatial Language

### Z-axis = consciousness layer

| Depth | Consciousness Layer | Treatment |
|-------|-------------------|-----------|
| Foreground | Performing mind (Vijnanamaya) | Sharp, cold, over-lit |
| Mid-ground | Emotional layer (Manomaya) | Softer, warmer, edges blur |
| Background | Felt sense (Pranamaya) | Warm glow, out of focus but FELT |
| Ground plane | Body/consciousness ground | Solid, warm, unmoving, everything settles here |

### Light Model

- Surface: cold ambient, dim blue-grey, flat, no shadows
- Descent: warm radial from below, deeper = warmer
- Light source = the ground itself
- Shadows fall UPWARD (the inversion)
- Inner child in Scene 05 GLOWS — it IS the light source

### The Breathing Grid

- Surface: tight, rigid, pixel-perfect (the performing mind)
- Mid-descent: looser, asymmetry enters, control relaxes
- Deep: no grid, organic placement
- Ground: centered, simple, one thing in vast space

## Pixel Art System

Why pixel art:
1. Language of the screen-native generation — 8-bit IS childhood
2. Pixels are honest — on or off, no anti-aliasing, no performing smoothness
3. Simplicity carries weight — viewer projects themselves into simple figures
4. Triggers pre-verbal memory — the feeling before the performance started

Rules:
- **Character:** 8-14px tall, no face detail, just silhouette. Viewer IS the character.
- **Inner child:** Same character, 60% size, warmer color, softer
- **Environment:** More detailed than character. Rich world, simple figure.
- **Palette:** 8-12 colors per scene. Constraint = clarity.
- **No outline on character** — exists through color contrast, not borders

## Color as Temperature

The palette is a temperature map. Cold at the surface, warm at the ground.

```
SURFACE (cold)                          GROUND (warm)
  #0a0a14 ── #0e0e18 ── #0a0814 ── #0a0804 ── #1a1208
  blue-black   slate     deep blue   warm black  dark amber
```

**The rule:** Once warm color enters (Scene 02), it never fully leaves. Warmth accumulates. You can't unsee it once you've seen it.

## Motion as Breath

| Motion | Meaning | Usage |
|--------|---------|-------|
| Fade from deep | Emergence into awareness | Text reveals, character appearances |
| Settle | Gravity, landing | Elements overshoot then settle |
| Dissolve | Letting go, control releasing | Scene transitions |
| Slow pulse | Life, vitality (4-6s cycle) | Inner child glow, cave warmth |
| Parallax drift | Layers at different speeds | Background vs foreground |

**Never:** bounce, spin, slide from side, or loop (except breath pulse).

## Typography as Architecture

| Layer | Font | Role |
|-------|------|------|
| Display/Emotional | Serif with character | Truth statements, wound-naming lines |
| Body/Naming | Clean sans (Space Grotesk) | Scene labels, descriptions |
| Pixel/System | Press Start 2P | Numbers, scroll hints, interface elements |

Text rules:
- Text lives IN the scene, not ON it
- One idea per screen, max two lines
- Text enters slowly (word-by-word, 0.08-0.12s)
- Text exits before next scene
- Vertical text as structure along edges

## Responsive: Mobile IS Primary

90% of audience is mobile. The phone in the dark at 2am.
- Portrait orientation native to vertical descent
- Pixel art scales beautifully (IS low-resolution)
- Generous text sizes, fewer words per line
- The scroll journey is BETTER on mobile

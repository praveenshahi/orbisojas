# Amoris Content Strategy
## The Core Problem This Solves

**Current:** Content starts inside the framework. Kosha, Vayu, Bhairava, transmission. Requires the person to already be 70% there to receive it.

**Fix:** Start where the person IS. Which is: confused, exhausted, vaguely feeling the world is broken, carrying something heavy they can't name, suspicious that the systems failed them, lonely in ways they can't explain.

That's where this conversation started. That's where every piece of content starts now.

---

## The Funnel That Already Exists Naturally

This is the journey of the conversation that converts — proven in real time:

```
1. MACRO CHAOS (world is broken, systems exploited us)
        ↓
2. PATTERN RECOGNITION (this was designed, not accidental)
        ↓
3. CONSCIOUSNESS AS BATTLEFIELD (attention is the real resource)
        ↓
4. THE INTERNAL SPLIT (fighting yourself = fighting the system inside)
        ↓
5. FEAR / SHAME / GUILT as the control OS
        ↓
6. THE WOUND ISN'T YOURS (parents' shame, lineage encoding)
        ↓
7. AMORIS — the container for this specific work
```

Every piece of content lives somewhere on this funnel. Never skip steps. Never start at 6 when the person is at 1.

## The One Rule

**Speak from where the user is standing. Not from where you've arrived.**

The user is standing in:
- A job that feels hollow
- Relationships that feel performed
- A body that feels like a liability
- A world that feels like a trap
- A self that feels like a costume

They are NOT standing in Kosha architecture. Yet.

---

## Content Archetypes — The 5 Entry Doors

Each door reaches a different segment of the same ICP. Use all five in rotation.

### Door 1 — The System Door
*For the person who sees the exploitation but can't name their internal response to it*

Hook pattern: "The system didn't fail you. It worked exactly as designed. The question is what it designed you to feel about yourself."

Topics: hollow achievement, corporate identity, financial anxiety, why the career feels like a performance

### Door 2 — The Relationship Door  
*For the person whose relationships feel like transactions or ego management*

Hook pattern: "You're not bad at relationships. You're good at performing them. Those are opposite problems."

Topics: loneliness in connection, intimacy avoidance, the partner as completion-seeking, why vulnerability feels dangerous

### Door 3 — The Body Door
*For the person who lives from the neck up*

Hook pattern: "Your body has been keeping score of everything your mind refused to feel."

Topics: anxiety in the chest, shame in the gut, the body as the first site of wound encoding, why thinking harder doesn't solve it

### Door 4 — The Parent Door
*For the person ready to look at lineage*

Hook pattern: "You didn't inherit your parents' trauma. You inherited their unfinished business. There's a difference — and the difference is where the work begins."

Topics: generational patterns, why you repeat what you hated, shame that doesn't feel like yours, the child who learned to disappear

### Door 5 — The World Door
*This conversation — macro to micro, the one that opened everything*

Hook pattern: "The chaos outside is the chaos inside. Same system. Same architecture. Same medicine."

Topics: geopolitical breakdown, consciousness as the battlefield, quiet non-participation, building real reciprocity

## The Three Mirror Questions as Content

These three questions ARE the content engine. Each one is a standalone post, a thread, a reel hook, a circle opening.

> *"What did you know about yourself as a child that the world taught you was wrong?"*

> *"Where in your body do you feel the weight of something that doesn't belong to you?"*

> *"What would you do if you knew the shame wasn't yours?"*

Post one question. Nothing else. No explanation. No Amoris mention. Watch who answers. Those are your people self-selecting in real time.

Rotate these monthly. The comments ARE your market research and your next 30 days of content.

---

## Tone Guardrails

**Always:**
- Start in the user's reality, not your framework
- Use plain language until the person is ready for the precise language
- Let the depth reveal itself through the conversation, not through the introduction
- One idea per piece. Fully inhabited. Not a list of ideas half-inhabited.

**Never:**
- Lead with Sanskrit terms to someone who hasn't asked
- Explain what Amoris is before they've felt what Amoris addresses
- Write from content-strategy brain — only write after sadhana, from Vijnanamaya
- Perform depth. Transmit it or don't post.

## The Conversion Mechanic

Content → Comment/DM → Mirror question conversation → Mirror product → Blueprint → Circle

The content never sells Amoris. It creates the felt need that Amoris answers. By the time someone reaches the Mirror, they already know this is what they were looking for. They just needed the language.

**The depth is the filter. The question is the door. The conversation is the conversion.**

# Amoris First 100 — Outreach Strategy

## The Premise

Don't find people who want Amoris.
Find people who are already living the problem Amoris solves.
They're everywhere. They just don't have the language yet.

The outreach doesn't introduce Amoris. It starts a conversation.
The conversation introduces itself.

---

## Who You're Actually Looking For

**Primary signal — they're already saying things like:**
- "I've done the therapy, read the books, still feel the same"
- "I built everything I was supposed to and feel empty"
- "I can see the patterns but I can't stop repeating them"
- "I don't know why I feel so alone even around people"
- "Something happened in childhood I've never fully dealt with"
- "The world feels broken and I feel like I'm the only one seeing it"

**Secondary signal:**
- Interested in Jungian psychology, Vedanta, breathwork, somatic work, astrology
- Questioning career, questioning relationships simultaneously
- Urban India 25-38
- Reads and thinks — not just consumes

**Where they are:**
- LinkedIn (hollow achiever content resonates with them)
- Instagram comments on depth content (Gabor Maté, Carl Jung accounts etc.)
- Reddit (r/India, r/meditation, r/psychotherapy, r/awakened)
- Twitter/X philosophy threads
- Substack readers of adjacent writers

## The Outreach Conversation Arc

This is not a sales sequence. It's the natural conversation that converts — same as the one that built this document.

### Stage 1 — The Opening (content or direct)
Start with Door 1-5 content (see Content Strategy doc).
OR direct message starting from their reality:

*"Saw your comment on [X post]. What you said about [specific thing] — that's not a small observation. Most people don't see it that way."*

Nothing about Amoris. Just genuine recognition of their Viveka.

### Stage 2 — The Conversation
Let it go where it goes. Don't steer toward Amoris.
If they have genuine Viveka, the conversation will naturally arrive at:
- Why the world feels the way it does
- What they're carrying that doesn't feel like theirs
- What they've tried that hasn't worked

Your job: be present. Ask one real question. Listen to the answer.

### Stage 3 — The Mirror Question
When the moment is right — not forced — drop one of the three:

*"Can I ask you something? What did you know about yourself as a child that the world taught you was wrong?"*

Their answer tells you everything. Quality of answer = quality of Viveka = readiness for Mirror.

### Stage 4 — The Natural Offer
If the conversation has genuine depth, the offer makes itself:

*"There's something I've been building specifically for this. Not therapy, not coaching — something more precise. Would you be open to a diagnostic conversation? No cost. Just to see if it fits."*

That diagnostic conversation IS the Mirror. They experience it before they know what it is.

### Stage 5 — Post-Mirror
After the Mirror conversation, two outcomes:
- They're not ready: they stay in the field, consume content, come back when ready
- They're ready: they move to Blueprint naturally

No chasing. No follow-up sequences. No urgency tactics.
The work is real. It will wait for the right person.

## The 100 People Math

You don't need 100 to convert. You need 100 genuine conversations.

Conversion rate from genuine Mirror conversation to Blueprint: conservatively 30-40% if the conversation had real depth.

So:
- 100 genuine conversations
- 30-40 Blueprint conversions at INR 2,000-5,000
- = INR 60,000 - 2,00,000 from Phase 1 alone
- + Circle cohort emerging from the 100 (10-15 ready for Level 3)
- = First Circle at INR 15,000-25,000 per person × 10 = INR 1.5-2.5 lakhs

That's the first real revenue number. From 100 conversations. Not 100,000 followers.

## Weekly Outreach Rhythm

| Activity | Time | Volume |
|----------|------|--------|
| Respond to Mirror question post comments personally | 30 min daily | All genuine responses |
| Find 5 people saying the signal phrases on LinkedIn/Reddit | 20 min daily | 5/day = 35/week |
| Send 3 genuine opening messages | 15 min daily | 3/day = 21/week |
| Have 1-2 deeper conversations | 30-45 min | 7-10/week |
| Offer Mirror to 2-3 ready people | Within conversation | 2-3/week |

At this rhythm: 100 genuine conversations in 10-12 weeks.

---

## What Not To Do

- Don't pitch Amoris in the opening message. Ever.
- Don't send the same message to multiple people. They feel it.
- Don't follow up more than once if no response.
- Don't explain the framework before they've felt the problem.
- Don't rush to the offer. The conversation IS the product at this stage.
- Don't target people who are comfortable. Look for the ones quietly asking real questions.

---

## The Community Before the Product

Before the first paid Circle, build the free field:

A WhatsApp or Telegram group. Not a broadcast channel. An actual conversation space.

Seed it with the first 20-30 Mirror conversations who aren't ready to pay but are genuine seekers.

Post one Mirror question per week. Let the conversation happen. Don't moderate heavily. Let the field build itself.

This group becomes:
- Your research lab (what's actually alive in the ICP right now)
- Your social proof engine (people experiencing real shifts before any paid product)
- Your first Circle waitlist (they'll ask when the Circle is available)

**The community IS the product at Phase 1. The paid products emerge from the community, not the other way around.**

Not conversion rate. Not revenue. Not followers.

**How many people in the world now carry a piece of genuine recognition they didn't have before the conversation?**

Each one of those is a transmission node. Each one creates the next conversation without you in the room.

That's how the hundred becomes a thousand without a marketing budget.

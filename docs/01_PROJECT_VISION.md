# 01 — Project Vision

## The one sentence

Someone arrives carrying a question they have never been able to answer. Within ninety seconds they see that question written on the screen, alongside the questions that sit next to it, and they understand for the first time that it is not a personal failing but a **pattern with a structure**. They request a Soul Mirror.

Everything in this repository serves that sentence. Any element that does not is removed.

## What Orbis Ojas is

Orbis Ojas is a consciousness operating system: a body of work that helps people see the architecture underneath their own behaviour. It is not a coaching practice, not a therapy service, not an astrology consultancy, and not a wellness brand. It is closer to cartography — the discipline of rendering something real, complex and previously unmapped in a form a person can actually navigate.

**Soul Mirror** is the first instrument. **The Orbis Atlas** will become the public knowledge layer. The website exists to make the first one desired and the second one inevitable.

## What Soul Mirror is

A deeply personalised report, roughly 32–40 pages, produced from a person's birth data plus a small number of reflective answers. It is analysed across multiple frameworks, refined by human interpreters, and delivered as a typeset document with an optional 1:1 integration session.

It contains:

- **A pattern map** — the person's standing across mind, identity, emotions, body, relationships and purpose.
- **A core loop** — the specific trigger → reaction → reinforcement → return cycle they are actually running.
- **A root story** — the belief underneath the loop.
- **Body impact** — where the loop lives physically.
- **Practices** — personalised, specific, sequenced. Not generic advice.

It does not predict the future. It describes present structure. That distinction is the brand's spine, and it is what separates Orbis Ojas from every adjacent category.

## Who it is for

People who are competent and largely successful, who have already consumed a great deal of self-knowledge, and who are frustrated that understanding has not produced change. They are not looking for motivation, and they are past the point where generic advice reads as insight. They are searching in first person: *why do I keep doing this?*

They are the least well-served group in this market, because the market sells either information (which they already have) or reassurance (which they do not want).

## The strategic position

Orbis Ojas occupies a gap between two vocabularies that describe the same human phenomena and almost never speak to each other:

| Western clinical / psychological | Eastern contemplative |
| --- | --- |
| attachment style | relational samskara |
| limiting belief | samskara |
| nervous-system dysregulation | vayu imbalance |
| somatic holding | annamaya kosha |
| shadow work | tamas / avidya |
| core wound | root impression |

Both have enormous public demand. Almost nothing credibly joins them. Orbis Ojas is the translation layer — a position that is simultaneously the product thesis, the content strategy and the search moat. See `05_SEO_STRATEGY.md` and `06_AEO_STRATEGY.md`.

## Design thesis

> If Apple built a consciousness operating system.

Practically, this means:

**Restraint carries the weight.** The subject matter is inherently prone to visual excess — mandalas, glows, purple gradients, cosmic wallpaper. We use almost none of it. A dark editorial ground, warm ivory serif type, one gold accent, and a great deal of space. The seriousness of the design is the argument that the content is serious.

**Esoteric content is rendered as taxonomy.** Koshas and vayus are presented the way a scientific atlas presents a classification — labelled, precise, editorially typeset. Never as decoration, never as mystique. Gold geometry appears only where it carries information.

**The interface is a mirror, not a brochure.** The homepage's first move is not to explain the company. It is to show the visitor their own question. Recognition precedes explanation, always.

## The elimination test

Every section, component and sentence must pass this:

> Could this appear on the site of a life coach, a therapist, a meditation app, an astrology service, or an AI SaaS company?

If yes, it is deleted or rewritten until the answer is no. This is applied literally during review, not held as a vague sentiment.

## Success criteria

**Primary.** Rate of visitors who reach `/soul-mirror/request` and complete it. This is the only conversion the site has.

**Secondary.**
- Lighthouse ≥ 99 on all four categories, mobile and desktop
- Site cited by name in AI answers for Tier-2 bridge queries within two quarters
- Tier-1 pattern queries indexed and ranking within one quarter
- A visitor can describe what Soul Mirror actually is after thirty seconds on the homepage

**Anti-goals.** Newsletter volume, time-on-site, social sharing, page count. None of these are optimised for, and pursuing them would damage the primary metric.

## Scope

Three pages: Homepage, Soul Mirror (plus its request form), Atlas. The About/Founder page is designed for and deliberately deferred. The architecture assumes the Atlas becomes a large interactive application; nothing built now may obstruct that.

## Non-negotiables

1. No fabricated social proof. Testimonials, ratings and counts ship only if they are real.
2. All copy server-rendered and present with JavaScript disabled.
3. No third-party script that is not load-bearing for the conversion.
4. WCAG AA, verified by hand, not by plugin.
5. Copy is written to a keyword target chosen in advance — and reads as though it were not.
